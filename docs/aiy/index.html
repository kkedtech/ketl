AI Harness Masterclass · Ages 18–24

From Code Consumers to System Architects.

Most AI education for young adults teaches prompting. We teach architecture. If you've learned a clever prompt, you've learned a trick. If you learn to build a harness - memory, tools, permissions, sensors, observability - you've learned how real systems are governed.

Built on two decades of building enterprise systems - not a weekend of AI enthusiasm. Part of the KETL pathway, Sydney, Australia.

The position

Agent = Model + Harness. We treat you as capable of governing a system - not as someone who needs to be protected from one.

The pathway

Explorer → Co-Creator → Architect.

A system to click into, not a syllabus to read. Each tier ends on what you build, not what you learn about.

01 Foundational Explorer ▸

Breaking the "search engine" habit. Precision prompting, understanding LLM mechanics, and working inside an ethical sandbox.

Milestone built "The Ultimate Researcher" - a prompt architecture that outputs verified citations and outlines, not pre-written essays.
02 Intermediate Co-Creator ▸

AI as a daily work Co-Creator and cognitive augmentation. The Socratic configuration, data translation, iterative co-creation.

Milestone built "The Thought Partner / Co-Creator" - a persistent AI persona tuned to a specific hobby or skill (chess analysis, photography critique, a debate sparring partner).
03 Mastery Architect ▸

Harness engineering. Building tools and APIs the AI is permitted to trigger, physical computing loops (virtual and physical electronic sensors), and guardrails you define yourself.

Milestone built "The Agentic Loop" - an agent receives a trigger, gathers data via a tool, decides within guardrails, and outputs a result.

Who it's for

Whichever door you're coming through.

Self-directed learners

This fits how you already like to learn.

Independence and ownership don't stop at technology. A harness - permissions, guardrails, observability - is a way of taking real ownership of an AI system, not just using one someone else built.

New to AI development

Your worry about AI dependency is the right worry.

Most AI tools train you to depend on an answer you didn't build. This pathway trains the opposite: understanding the system well enough to govern it, not just query it.

Questions we get asked.

+Isn't this just prompt engineering with a fancier name?

No. Prompting is one skill inside Explorer. Architect is about the harness around the model - the tools it's permitted to call, the guardrails it must respect, and the observability that lets you see what it actually did.

+Do I need coding experience?

No. Explorer assumes none. By Architect, you're building small tools and APIs - the pathway builds toward that rather than requiring it upfront.

+Who's actually teaching this?

Live mentorship from someone who has built enterprise systems professionally for two decades - this pathway is that experience brought down to something you can build and understand.

+What if I'm not sure this is right for me yet?

Start with the free intro session. No paid commitment - just a clear look at the pathway before you decide anything.
