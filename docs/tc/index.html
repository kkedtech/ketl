4. Cancellations, Refunds & Transfers

  • More than 14 days before the program start date: full refund, less any payment-processing fees.
  • Within 14 days of the start date: 50% refund, or full credit toward a future cohort.
  • Within 48 hours of the start date, or after the program has started: no refund; credit toward a future cohort may be offered at KETL's discretion.
  • If KETL cancels or reschedules a cohort (e.g. insufficient enrolments, facilitator unavailability), enrolled participants receive a full refund or transfer to an alternative cohort, their choice.
  • Missed individual sessions within a multi-session program (e.g. one day of an 8-day sprint) are not individually refundable; where possible, we'll offer a catch-up resource or recording.
