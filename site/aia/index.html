AI Harness Masterclass · Ages 12–18+

From Code Consumers to System Architects.

Most AI education for teenagers teaches prompting. We teach architecture. A student who learns a clever prompt has learned a trick. A student who learns to build a harness - memory, tools, permissions, sensors, observability - has learned how real systems are governed.

Built on two decades of building enterprise systems - not a weekend of AI enthusiasm. Part of the KETL pathway, Sydney, Australia.

The position

Agent = Model + Harness. We treat students as near-adults capable of governing a system - not children being protected from one.

The pathway

Explorer → Co-Creator → Architect.

A system to click into, not a syllabus to read. Each tier ends on what a student builds, not what they learn about.

01 Foundational Explorer ▸

Breaking the "search engine" habit. Precision prompting, understanding LLM mechanics, and working inside an ethical sandbox.

Milestone built "The Ultimate Researcher" - a prompt architecture that outputs verified citations and outlines, not pre-written essays.
02 Intermediate Co-Creator ▸

AI as a daily work Co-Creator and cognitive augmentation. The Socratic configuration, data translation, iterative co-creation.

Milestone built "The Thought Partner / Co-Creator" - a persistent AI persona tuned to a specific hobby or skill (chess analysis, photography critique, a debate sparring partner).
03 Mastery Architect ▸

Harness engineering. Building tools and APIs the AI is permitted to trigger, physical computing loops (virtual and physical electronic sensors), and guardrails the student defines themselves.

Milestone built "The Agentic Loop" - an agent receives a trigger, gathers data via a tool, decides within guardrails, and outputs a result.

Montessori-aligned families

This fits the pedagogy you already trust.

Independence and the prepared environment don't stop at technology. A harness - permissions, guardrails, observability - is a prepared environment for AI. Your child sets the boundaries; they don't just work inside someone else's.

Parents new to this

Your worry about screen time and dependency is the right worry.

Most AI tools train a child to depend on an answer they didn't build. This pathway trains the opposite: a student who understands the system well enough to govern it, not just query it.

Questions we get asked.

+Isn't this just prompt engineering with a fancier name?

No. Prompting is one skill inside Explorer. Architect is about the harness around the model - the tools it's permitted to call, the guardrails it must respect, and the observability that lets a student see what it actually did.

+Does my child need coding experience?

No. Explorer assumes none. By Architect, students are building small tools and APIs - the pathway builds toward that rather than requiring it upfront.

+Who's actually teaching this?

Live mentorship from someone who has built enterprise systems professionally for two decades - this pathway is that experience brought down to something a 12–18 year old can build and understand.

+What if we're not sure this is right for our child yet?

Start with the free session for parents and educators. No paid commitment - just a clear look at the pathway before you decide anything.
